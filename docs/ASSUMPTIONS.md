# Assumptions to confirm

Nothing in this list is an official AICTE rule. Each item must be checked against the applicable Approval Process Handbook (APH) and AICTE procedures before any real use.

## Workflow

1. **Routes.** The five templates (Only Scrutiny, Scrutiny + EVC, EVC + SAC/SHC style routes, Only EVC, and a longer re-scrutiny route) are illustrative. Confirm the actual stage sequence per category.
2. **Category names.** "Infrastructure revision", "Program extension", "Re-verification visit", "New institution" and "Annual compliance" are demonstration labels, not official categories.
3. **Stage owners.** Bureau, reviewer, EVC coordinator and competent authority are the stage owners. Confirm which body performs each stage.
4. **Checklists.** The checklist items are generic examples. The mandatory documents and verification points must come from the APH.
5. **Return path.** A return keeps the application at the same stage. The APH may require return to an earlier stage.
6. **Decisions.** The three outcomes (approved, deferred, rejected) are placeholders. The statutory decision set and its effects are not modelled.

## Timelines and thresholds

7. **Stage target days** (15, 10, 30, 20) are illustrative.
8. **EVC target** is 14 days after the EVC stage starts. This is not an official EVC timeline.
9. **Undertaking reminders** at 30, 15, 7 and 0 days before the due date are illustrative.
10. **Document reminders** at 30, 15, 7 and 0 days before expiry, as the brief suggests, are illustrative defaults.
11. **Review turnaround** of 3 days for evidence and renewals is illustrative.
12. **Escalation delays** (3, 5 and 7 days per level) and the two-level ladder are illustrative.

## Roles and authority

13. **Role names** (Administrator, Regional officer, Bureau officer, EVC coordinator, Committee reviewer, Competent authority, Auditor, Institution user) are proposed, not official designations.
14. **Regional supervisor** and **designated administrative authority** are the escalation targets. Confirm the real escalation chain.
15. **Scope.** Institution users see their institution. Regional officers see their region. Confirm whether bureau or committee members should be scoped as well.

## Documents and evidence

16. **Accepted file types** are PDF, PNG and JPG up to 10 MB. Confirm the formats and size limits in the procedure.
17. **Document categories** (lease deed, building safety certificate, land-use permission, fire-safety certificate) are examples. The mandatory list must come from the APH.

## Data and integration

18. **No real data.** Seed data is fictional. Place names are used for flavour only.
19. **No AICTE system is connected.** Any integration would need an official API, authorisation and credentials.
20. **Retention.** The prototype does not define retention or deletion periods for audit records or files.
21. **Encryption at rest** depends on the storage used in production. PGlite on a local disk does not provide it.

## Notifications

22. **Email and SMS** are not configured. The in-app notification centre is the only channel.
