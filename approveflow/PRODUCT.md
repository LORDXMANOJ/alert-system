# ApproveFlow

## What it is
An operations console for technical-education approvals (SIH26262, AICTE): applications move through scrutiny, EVC visits and a competent-authority decision; institutions keep time-bound undertakings and prescribed documents (leases) valid; a deadline engine raises alerts and escalates overdue work.

## Who uses it
Regional and bureau officers, EVC coordinators, committee reviewers, the competent authority, auditors, and institution staff. Hackathon judges will see it live. People scan it many times a day, so it must read at a glance.

## Mode
Operate. The task is completed on the screen: see what is late, act on it, move on.

## Tone
Calm, exact, confident. A console, not a marketing page. Quiet surfaces, one strong signal colour for what needs attention. Words only where a glance is not enough; detail lives in hover tooltips.

## Fixed brief
- Palette: Tea Green #C1F7DC, Pale Sky #C3D2D5, Lilac #BDA0BC, Dusty Mauve #A2708A, Grape Soda #824670. Dark theme.
- No rounded card containers. Group with space, hairlines and type.
- Status is shown with colour (marks, bars), not pill badges with sentences. Hover reveals the detail.
- Distinctive typography; no Inter/Geist defaults.
- Login, a short loading moment after sign-in, and smooth page transitions with a signature motion.
- Images may appear but never show people's faces.
- It must feel like a real product: realistic fictional records, no "demo" banners in the UI.

## Must stay true
Automation never approves, rejects or closes a case. Decisions stay with the competent authority. Permissions and record scope are enforced on the server.
